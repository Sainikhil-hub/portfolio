MNIT Jaipur Tech Fest — SPHINX '24

Participant • RC Aircraft & Aeromodelling • MNIT Jaipur, Rajasthan (Nov 2024)

MNIT Jaipur SPHINX '24 RC Aircraft Aeromodelling RAIoT Club Tech Fest Exposure

Represented RAIoT Club (Amity University Rajasthan) at SPHINX '24, the annual technical festival of MNIT Jaipur, in November 2024. Built and test-flew RC aircraft prototypes, getting hands-on with aeromodelling fundamentals — airframe design, fabrication and flight testing — while exploring the fest and the MNIT Jaipur campus with the team.

What I learned: hands-on aeromodelling — how airframe design, weight balance and control surfaces decide whether an RC aircraft actually flies — and the confidence of representing my team at an NIT tech fest.
